A few things to know.

Can I use Pitchmade today?

Not yet. We’re building the product and using this waitlist to understand what creators need. Joining saves your place for updates; it does not create an account or unlock the product.

Who is it for?

We’re starting with YouTube creators who pitch brands themselves. TikTok, Instagram, and UGC creators are welcome to register interest and tell us what they need, but those workflows are not promised for the first release.

Will it send pitches or promise brand deals?

No. You stay in control of the pitch and send it yourself. Pitchmade is intended to help you present your case clearly; it cannot guarantee replies or deals.

What happens after I join?

Your email is added to the waitlist. We plan to send an early-access update and may ask for feedback on what we’re building. There’s no launch date or product price announced yet, and no payment is collected here.

How can I leave the waitlist?

After a new signup, we show a private management link. Save it to remove your email and optional feedback. Any future emails will also include a way to opt out.

Your email has one job.

We collect your email to contact you about Pitchmade early access and occasional product feedback. If you choose to share your platform or pitching challenge, we keep that alongside your signup to understand what to build.

We also record the signup time, your consent, and basic campaign tags from the page link. We do not use advertising trackers or sell the waitlist.

Signups are stored on the server, not just in your browser. The signup form cannot read other people’s details. Short-lived hashed network identifiers help limit spam; they are not used for advertising.

Your private management link lets you delete your signup and feedback. Keep it private. If you clear your browser, use the copy you saved. Future emails will include an opt-out.

We keep your signup until you remove it or the waitlist closes. Joining is not a purchase, and it does not grant access to the unfinished product.
